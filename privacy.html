Privacy Policy — Contractor's Cleanout

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Contractor's Cleanout is made by Bethel Ventures. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

Contractor's Cleanout has no servers, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. We do not collect, sell, share, or transmit any of your information.

How the app works

  • You post listings for leftover materials — FOR SALE, FREE, or HAUL-OFF JOBS (where you pay a hauler to take a pile away) — right on your phone. Every listing you add — its title, description, category, deal type, price or pay amount, and photos — stays on your device.
  • Pile Check answers three quick questions about your pile and suggests the smart move: sell it, post it free, or pay a hauler. Pile Check runs entirely on your device. Nothing about your pile, your photo, or your answers is sent anywhere.
  • You browse listings saved on your device, and you contact a poster directly by phone, using your own phone app. The app does not place the call for you and does not record who you call.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your listings are stored in a private database on your phone only.
  • Photos you add to a listing are copied into the app's private storage on your phone only. They are never uploaded, emailed, or transmitted anywhere.
  • When you delete a listing, its photos are deleted with it. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Camera and photos

  • The app uses your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo to one of your listings. It never accesses your camera or photos on its own.
  • Pile Check looks at your photo on your device to help suggest a deal. Nothing about the photo leaves your phone.

What the app does not do

  • It does not take payments or ask for bank, card, or account information.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, microphone, or messages.
  • It does not show ads and contains no advertising or tracking software.
  • It does not collect, share, or transmit any information about you or your listings.

Children: Contractor's Cleanout is made for adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes: If a future version changes how the app handles information, this policy will be updated and the new version posted at the same address before the change takes effect.
